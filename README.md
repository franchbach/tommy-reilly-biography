# A Complete Book For Tommy Reilly

**A biography of Tommy Reilly (1919–2000), the legendary Scottish-Canadian chromatic harmonica player** — written in Chinese, with English translation (machine-translated draft). German and Japanese translations are planned.

汤米·赖利（Tommy Reilly, 1919–2000）——二十世纪最伟大的半音阶口琴演奏家之一。本书是他的中文传记（franchbach 著），并附机器翻译英文初稿；德文、日文译本规划中。

## About the Book / 关于本书

Tommy Reilly elevated the harmonica from a street instrument to the classical concert stage. Over his six-decade career, more than thirty composers — including Gordon Jacob, Robert Farnon, James Moody and George Martin — wrote works for him. This book covers:

- 早年岁月：从加拿大到英国（1919–1939）/ Early years: Canada to the UK
- 战俘营岁月（1939–1945）/ The prisoner-of-war years
- 战后重建与技法科学 / Post-war technique and style
- 第一首口琴协奏曲与音乐分析 / The first harmonica concerto (Spivakovsky)
- 黄金年代：委托创作与作品深度解析 / The golden age of commissions
- 银口琴的诞生（1967）/ The silver harmonica
- 口琴教育奠基与传承（TRIC 国际口琴俱乐部）/ Teaching legacy: TRIC
- 录音、演出与跨界合作 / Recordings, performances
- 主要唱片目录与研究 / Discography
- 二十世纪口琴艺术地图 / The harmonica world of the 20th century
- 附录 A–G：年表、作品列表、唱片目录、参考文献、语录集、演出足迹、考据笔记 / Appendices
