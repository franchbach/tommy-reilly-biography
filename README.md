# A Complete Book For Tommy Reilly

**A biography of Tommy Reilly (1919–2000), the legendary Scottish-Canadian chromatic harmonica player** — written in Chinese, with a full English translation (machine-translated draft). German and Japanese translations are planned.

![Tommy Reilly (1919-2000), chromatic harmonica master](zh/assets/img_008.jpeg)

*Tommy Reilly (1919–2000), master of the chromatic harmonica. Studio portrait from the 1940s–50s, with his autograph.*

## About the Book

Tommy Reilly elevated the harmonica from a street instrument to the classical concert stage. Over his six-decade career, more than thirty composers — including Gordon Jacob, Robert Farnon, James Moody and George Martin — wrote works for him. This book covers:

- **Early Years**: Canada to the UK (1919–1939)
- **The Prisoner-of-War Years** (1939–1945)
- **Post-War Reconstruction**: technique and style (1945–1951)
- **Breakthrough**: the first harmonica concerto and musical analysis (1951)
- **The Golden Age**: commissions and in-depth work analysis (1951–1970)
- **The Silver Harmonica**: instrument innovation (1967)
- **Teaching Legacy**: TRIC International Harmonica Club
- **Recordings, Performances and Cross-Genre Collaborations**
- **Discography**: major recordings and research
- **The Harmonica World of the 20th Century**: Reilly and his contemporaries
- **Appendices A–G**: timeline, commissioned works, discography, references, quotations, performance venues, fact-checking notes
