# Appendix A: Tommy Reilly Timeline

| Year | Event |
| August 21, 1919 | Born in Guelph, Ontario, Canada |
| 1927 | Began violin lessons (age 8) |
| 1930 | Joined the Elmdale Harmonica Band founded by his father |
| 1932-1933 | Won consecutive gold medals at the Canadian National Exhibition |
| 1935 | Family moved to the UK |
| 1937-1939 | European tour with "The Four Phillips" |
| 1939 | Entered the Leipzig Conservatory; in September, arrested by the Gestapo |
| 1939-1945 | Imprisoned in a POW camp, technical system formed |
| 1945 | Returned to the UK, began BBC broadcasting career |
| c. 1947 | Began performing on BBC radio |
| 1948 | Met Michael Spivakovsky in Torquay, conceived concerto idea |
| May 26, 1951 | Premiere of Spivakovsky's *Harmonica Concerto* |
| 1953 | Successful at Stuttgart "Light Music Week"; recorded *Gypsy Airs* in Oslo |
| 1965 | Released Irish folk album *The Life of Reilly* (World Sound T 541) |
| 1966 | Premiered Farnon's *Prelude and Dance* |
| 1967 | Collaborated with Hohner to create the silver harmonica; founded Tommy Reilly International Club |
| 1968 | Released *Colours of My Life* (Polydor 184 107) |
| 1969 | *Midnight Cowboy* theme; co-authored textbook with James Moody published |
| 1971 | Tommy Reilly International Club ended |
| 1972 | Released French-themed album *Harmonica Parisien* (Polydor 2382 016) |
| 1976 | Released duet harmonica album *Music for Two Harmonicas* with Sigmund Groven |
| 1987 | Masterclass at World Harmonica Championships |
| 1992 | Awarded Member of the Order of the British Empire (MBE) |
| December 1993 | Involved in serious car accident, health deteriorated |
| 1995 | Released compilation *Tommy Reilly* (CPCD-07) by Classic People in Korea |
| 1996 | EMI Premier's *Vintage Themes* included his TV theme recordings |
| 1998 | Last professional masterclass (Dartington) |
| September 25, 2000 | Died in Farnham, Surrey, UK |
| 2019 | Released album *A Life in Music*, centenary celebration |
