## F.7 Major Music Festivals and Milestone Performances

- **May 26, 1951**: Royal Festival Hall, London, premiere of Spivakovsky's *Harmonica Concerto* at the Festival of Britain (BBC Concert Orchestra, conducted by Mark Lubbock, BBC live broadcast)

- **1953**: Success at Stuttgart's "Week of Light Music" opens doors to radio and TV invitations across Europe; records *Gypsy Airs* in Oslo (with live applause)

- **1966**: Concert celebrating the 20th anniversary of the Oslo, Norwegian Broadcasting Orchestra, attended by King Olav V, world premiere of Farnon's *Prelude and Dance*

- **June 1-2, 1976**: St John's Smith Square, London, recording of *Works for Harmonica and Orchestra* (Argo ZRG 856, conducted by Marriner)

- **1987**: Jersey International Harmonica Festival - One-hour Festival Concert (pieces included *Gymnopedie No.1*, *Jacaranda*, *The Swan*, *Duettino* (with Groven), *Lazy Lullaby*, *Toledo*) and 45-minute workshop

- **BBC Radio Concert**: Hof Altmarkt Arts Centre (program included *Five Bagatelles*, *Gymnopedie No.1*, *Bulgarian Wedding Dance*, *Toledo*, *The Swan*, medley from *Porgy and Bess*, *The Entertainer*, *Plaisir d'amour*, *Hava Nagila*)
